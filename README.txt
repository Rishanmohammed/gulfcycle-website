1. Replace the demo company name "GulfCycle" and logo with the real business identity.
2. Replace every +971 50 000 0000 / 971500000000 demo phone number with the real WhatsApp and telephone number.
3. Replace info@gulfcycle.ae with the real email address.
4. Update location, operating hours, accepted materials, service coverage, and any claims to match the actual company.
5. Add the real company logo and real yard/team photos.
6. Connect a backend or form service if you need direct email delivery and secure photo uploads. The current inquiry form opens WhatsApp and does not upload files automatically.
